**AI God-Mode Platform** is an idle simulation and cosmic reality management simulator designed for offline and casual entertainment.

## 2. Information We Process

### A. Local Storage & Device Data
The App uses local browser/webview storage () on your device solely to:
- Persist unlocked reality dimensions and stability ratings
- Save god-mode upgrades, cosmic ascension tiers, and prestige perks
- Preserve user preferences (audio toggles, particle effects, and visual settings)

All data remains client-side on your device and is never uploaded to remote servers. Clearing your application storage or uninstalling the app clears this local data.

### B. Network & Telemetry
The core application runs completely offline without requiring an active internet connection. We do not track user keystrokes, GPS location, contacts, or personal files.

### C. Optional Advertising & Monetization
If the App integrates standard advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob) for optional rewarded boosts:
- Third-party advertising networks may collect anonymous, non-personal device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID) solely to deliver non-intrusive ads, prevent ad fraud, and provide frequency capping.
- Please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details on their data processing practices.
